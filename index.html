☁️ Skybound Adventure

🪙 Coins: 0 ❤️ Lives: 3 🌎 Level: 1/3
Collect coins and reach the green flag!

📖 How to Play

🪙 Gold coins: Collect these to earn points!

🟩 Green platforms: Stand on these to travel safely.

🟨 Moving platforms: Ride these to reach higher places.

👾 Purple monsters: Avoid touching them from the side.

🚩 Green flag: Reach it to advance to the next level.

❤️ Lives: You lose a life when you fall or get hit.

🎒 Your character: Move and jump through the world.

🎮 Controls

◀ Move left

▶ Move right

⬆ JUMP to leap between platforms

↻ Restart the game from the beginning

💡 Tips

• Jump on monsters from above to destroy them without losing a life.

• Collect as many coins as possible.

• Watch moving platforms before jumping onto them.

• If you fall, you'll respawn at the starting platform.

Move with ◀ ▶ · Jump with ⬆ or Space · Avoid purple slimes!
